Why CAO

Why partners work with us

  • Strong networks

    CAO has established a strong network with civil society and government organizations, and is supported by civil society organizations and influential networks in the communities.

  • A committed team

    A qualified, multidisciplinary team whose members often devote extra time to the organization’s work.

  • Community trust

    CAO has a good reputation among the populations it serves, built through community-based intervention in urban and rural areas.

  • Local presence

    Our head office is in Herat province, with a presence in the central region in Kabul, and we intend to be active in every province of Afghanistan.
