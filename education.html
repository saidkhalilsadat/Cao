Education

Access to quality education and literacy

We increase access to quality education for individuals and communities through teacher capacity building, digital education, education in emergencies and literacy for adults.

Focus

  • Education and training for children without a guardian
  • Education for girls who are left out of school
  • Learning and professional opportunities for widows and poor women
  • Non-formal and adult education
  • Integrated programs for street and working children

Experience

  • Food for Education programs that help children stay in school
  • Distribution of school books
  • Construction of a school with nine classrooms
