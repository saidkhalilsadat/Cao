What we do

Five program areas, built around the needs of the communities we serve.

Our approach

Communities lead their own development

We work through community participation. Our team includes social science experts, psychologists, environmentalists, legal advisers and health personnel, together with community-level workers and peer educators.

Community Development

Institutions, capacity and local government

Health and Nutrition

Primary health care, nutrition and hygiene

Agriculture and Livestock

Food security, irrigation and natural resources

Education

Access to quality education and literacy

Disaster Management and Repatriation

Resilience, risk reduction and relief

Who we reach

Women, children, youth and senior citizens

Women

Empowerment, health education and livelihoods support.

Children

Education and protection, including children without a guardian.

Youth

Reproductive health, non-formal education and skills.

Senior citizens

Access to health and community support.
