About CAO

A registered Afghan NGO focused on community participation for sustainable development.

Our background

Working with communities since 1999

CAO – Towards Total Community Development – is a registered non-governmental organization established in 1999.

Since its establishment, CAO has implemented many projects funded by UN agencies and other international donors, particularly in the western region. The organization paused its work for some years and has recently restarted its activities with a qualified and energetic team.

Our aim is to improve the socio-economic status of underprivileged and disadvantaged women, older people, children and youth. Over time our work has grown to include access to health, education, basic rights, the environment and community sanitation.

Our registered office is in Herat. We intend to have an active presence in every province of Afghanistan.

Direction

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To reach out to the marginalized section of society, especially women, children and youth of the slums, and to empower underprivileged communities on gender and their rights through community participation for sustainable development.

Vision

To evolve innovative approaches and processes that use traditional skills and knowledge, making development projects more efficient and eco-friendly.

  • Networking
  • Transparency
  • Mutual respect
Focus

Areas of concern

  • Women’s empowerment
  • Training programs on birth spacing for women
  • Adolescent reproductive health and rights
  • Non-formal and adult education
  • Integrated programs for street and working children
  • Health, including diabetes, tuberculosis and AIDS
  • Sustainable environmental development and community sanitation
Approach

Our strategy

  • Providing health and medical services in remote villages and towns
  • Growing industry and handicrafts, especially professional services
  • Expanding education and training for children without a guardian, girls left out of education, and widows and poor women, and improving their educational and professional conditions
  • Improving agriculture and livestock
  • Performing other social services
People

Leadership

A multidisciplinary team of social science experts, psychologists, environmentalists, legal advisers and health personnel, supported by community-level workers and peer educators in urban and rural areas.
